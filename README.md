## 1. Why BeFocus?

| The problem | How BeFocus solves it |
|---|---|
| 📱 Hours lost every day to endless **Reels and Shorts** | Blocks only the Reels/Shorts feed. Messages and the rest of the app keep working. |

## 5. Allow restricted settings (Android 13+)

On **Android 13 and newer**, Android blocks Accessibility for apps installed from an APK. The BeFocus switch is greyed out, or you see a **"Restricted setting"** popup. You have to unlock it **once**:

1. **First try to turn on Accessibility** (BeFocus → Permissions → Accessibility → Allow → I agree → tap BeFocus). You'll get the *Restricted setting* popup. Tap **OK**.
   > ⚠️ Don't skip this. The *Allow restricted settings* option only appears **after** Android has blocked it once.
2. Open **Settings → Apps** (or *Apps → See all apps*) and tap **BeFocus**.
3. Tap the **⋮ three dots** in the **top-right corner**.
4. Tap **Allow restricted settings**.
5. Confirm with your **PIN, pattern or fingerprint**.
6. Go back to **Settings → Accessibility → BeFocus**. The switch now works.

## 7. Add an accountability partner

A partner makes it possible to unlock protection instantly when you *really* need to, without being able to do it alone.

1. Your friend installs an authenticator app (**Google Authenticator**, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy…).
2. They scan the **QR code** on your screen, or type the setup key.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** shown on their phone and tap **Confirm partner**.

Later, their current code unlocks your protection. No partner? Tap *Skip for now*; then unlocking always takes 48 hours.

## 9. Turning protection off

Adding protection is always allowed. **Removing** it (turning a shield off, deleting a schedule, uninstalling) needs an unlock. Open **Home → 🔒 Lock & unlock**:

- **Option 1 · Partner code:** enter the current 6-digit code from your friend's authenticator app, and it unlocks instantly.
- **Option 2 · Wait 48 hours:** start the timer and come back in 48 hours. Most cravings pass long before that.

<table><tr><td align="center"><img src="screenshots/49-unlock.png" width="190"><br>Lock &amp; unlock</td></tr></table>

**To uninstall BeFocus:** unlock first (above), turn off **Prevent uninstall** in Shields, then uninstall normally. If Device admin is still active, go to Settings → Security → Device admin apps → turn BeFocus off first.

## 10. Privacy

- BeFocus works **entirely on your phone**.
- It does **not** collect, store or share what you see or type. Text on screen is checked on the device only, to find blocked apps, websites and keywords.
- Password fields are never read.
- The only network use is ads (Google AdMob) on the focus screen and daily summary.

## 11. Troubleshooting / FAQ

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **"App not installed" / install blocked** | Remove any older BeFocus first. Use [Install anyway](#42-install-and-get-past-the-play-protect-warning), or [pause Play Protect](#43-only-if-there-is-no-install-anyway-button-pause-play-protect-scanning) and turn it back on afterwards. |
| **Accessibility switch greyed out / "Restricted setting"** | Do [step 5: Allow restricted settings](#5-allow-restricted-settings-android-13). Try turning Accessibility on once *before* looking for the ⋮ option. |
| **No "Allow restricted settings" in the ⋮ menu** | Try to enable Accessibility for BeFocus first, then reopen App info. |
| **Blocking stops after some time** | Set battery to **Unrestricted** and do the brand-specific steps in [6.4](#64-unrestricted-battery-keeps-it-running). After a phone update, check that Accessibility is still on. |
| **Accessibility keeps turning itself off** | Usually the battery saver is killing the app. See 6.4. On Xiaomi, also enable **Autostart**. |
| **Reels not blocked** | Make sure Shields → Reels & Shorts is on and the platform is selected. Apps change their layout often, so it is best-effort. Update BeFocus when a new version is out. |
| **Website not blocked in my browser** | Most major browsers are supported. Add the site to **My block list** too. |
| **Can I remove protection right now?** | Only with your partner's code, or after the 48-hour wait. That is the point. 🙂 |
